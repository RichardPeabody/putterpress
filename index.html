THE PERSON BEHIND THE PAGES

Meet the editor.

Alistair Dunsmuir, BA (Hons)

EDITOR · THE GOLF BUSINESS & GREENKEEPING

Alistair graduated in 1999 with a BA (Hons) degree in History, Politics and Philosophy from the University of Southampton. He first wrote about golf while editing Local Government News, covering the management of municipal golf courses. In 2005, he became only the eighth editor of Golf Club Management, a publication established in 1935.

That publication became The Golf Business in 2017. Today, Alistair edits both The Golf Business and GreenKeeping, covering the people, ideas and decisions shaping golf clubs and golf course management.
